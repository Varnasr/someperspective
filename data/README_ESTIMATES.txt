Supplementary CSV series, revised 10 October 2026.

Each file has the columns year, raw_value, estimate, lo, hi, method and notes.
Every row holds a published figure, and its notes name the publisher, document and date.
A year with no published figure is left empty or left out. Nothing is interpolated,
fitted or extended by a trend.

sanitation_odf_verified.csv     Rural household toilet coverage (%): Census 2011 and Swachh Bharat Mission (Gramin) MIS figures given to Parliament or by PIB.
state_finances_cess_share.csv   Cesses and surcharges as % of the Union's gross tax revenue: 15th Finance Commission and Union Receipt Budgets.
society_communal_incidents.csv  Communal incidents, 2004-2017: Ministry of Home Affairs counts. MHA has published none after 2017.

education_exam_events.csv and politics_opposition_incarceration.csv were removed because no published source gives either count year by year.
See METHODOLOGY.md for the details of each series.
