What each column is

Per-capita NSDP 2023-24 at current prices, 2011-12 series (MoSPI state accounts via api.mospi.gov.in, retrieved 10 October 2026; Haryana, which that table does not carry, from the Economic Survey 2023-24 Statistical Appendix Table 1.11A, as on 15 March 2024); current Lok Sabha seats (543-seat distribution frozen on the 1971 census); population as on 1 March 2026 from the National Commission on Population, 'Population Projections for India and States 2011-2036' (Report of the Technical Group on Population Projections, Registrar General of India), Table 21, converted from thousands to crore; 2011 Census population shown alongside for the comparison; total fertility rate (NFHS-5, 2019-21). 16 states covering about 90% of India's projected 2026 population and 477 of 543 Lok Sabha seats.

Representation here is computed on the official 2026 projection. The 2011 census is fifteen years stale and would understate how unequal representation has become. The projection is the government's own and is the best available number, but it is a projection because the 2021 census was postponed and the next enumeration is not due until February 2027. A census would replace it with a count. This project's argument applies here as well, because even measuring the fairness of a vote now depends on an estimate.

Return per ₹100 of Union taxes. Return per ₹100 of Union taxes paid, from Finance Commission devolution, as estimated by Praveen Chakravarty for the All India Professionals' Congress. Available for 8 of the 16 states. This project's author is a former Manmohan Singh Fellow of the AIPC, so this is a politically affiliated source on a politically contested question and is flagged as such: treat the direction as well established and the exact ratios as the AIPC's own estimates, which this project has not been able to trace to a published table. Rich states financing poorer ones is how the system is designed to work.

Projected seat change. Projected Lok Sabha seat change after the first census held post-2026 (Carnegie Endowment; ISAS; The India Forum). Available for 4 of the 16 states, and estimates vary considerably between them.
