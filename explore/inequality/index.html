Who receives the income

The shares of national income going to the top 1% and the bottom half, and the income of rural households.

Every chart runs from 2004 at the left edge to 2026 at the right and changes side between 2013 and 2014. Dashed lines are the two period averages. A gap is a year with no published figure.

Real growth in rural household income

2014-2026 average113.4%
Latest, 2021126.8%

Published from 2016. There is no earlier figure on the same basis, so the two periods cannot be compared on this series.

Source and method

NABARD All India Rural Financial Inclusion Survey (NAFIS), average monthly income of rural households: Rs 8,059 in 2016-17 and Rs 12,698 in 2021-22, a nominal rise of 57.6%. Deflated by MoSPI's CPI-Rural (base 2012=100; fiscal-year averages 132.43 and 164.53, a rise of 24.2%), the real rise is 26.8%, about 4.9% a year. Indexed 2016-17 = 100, so 2016 = 100 and 2021 = 126.8. NAFIS has two rounds only; every other year is left empty. The same survey found the share of rural households with outstanding debt rose from 47.4% to 52%.
