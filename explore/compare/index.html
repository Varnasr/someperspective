A tool

Compare two series

Choose any two series to plot one against the other, year by year. Teal points are 2004 to 2013 and violet points are 2014 to 2026; each point is labelled with its year where there is room, and every point names its year when you point at it.

A correlation between two annual series says nothing about cause. Read it as a prompt to look at the two charts side by side.
