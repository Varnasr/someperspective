The data

Every series, by topic

The site's data, one topic to a page, each drawn from data.json and the index script that the front page and the paper use. Every series names its publisher, and a year with no published figure is left empty.

Topics

Growth, prices and the budgetOutput, inflation, the Union's fiscal deficit and the exchange rate, year by year from 2004.WorkUnemployment, participation and the kind of work people do. Most of these series begin in 2017-18 with the Periodic Labour Force Survey, so they have no earlier counterpart on the same basis.Who receives the incomeThe shares of national income going to the top 1% and the bottom half, and the income of rural households.Press freedom and democratic qualityThe press freedom rank and the Democratic Quality Index, with the published scores behind it and India's position against four peers.Official statisticsThe Statistical Suppression Index scores documented events in six streams of official statistics. The events, their dates and the severity given to each are listed below the chart.The Union and the statesThe Fiscal Centralisation Index and the four published fiscal ratios and two coded events it is built from.The statesIncome, population, seats and transfers for the sixteen largest states.Compare two seriesPlot any two series against each other, year by year.The people behind the dataFour accounts from the author's fieldwork and the public record, beside the figures.Reading the Indian economyA primer on what the main numbers measure, how they are built and where they mislead.ForecastsWhat the RBI, the World Bank, the OECD, the ADB and the IMF expect for 2026-27 and 2027-28.Sources and methodThe publisher behind every series, and how the indices are built.
