A1. How the indices are constructed

General principles

All indices follow a standardized construction methodology:

Index_t = Σ(w_i × normalize(X_it)) / Σw_i where: X_it = Raw value of component i at time t w_i = Weight assigned to component i normalize() = Min-max normalization to [0,1]

Normalization formula

normalize(X) = (X - X_min) / (X_max - X_min) For reverse indicators: normalize_reverse(X) = 1 - normalize(X)

A2. Statistical Suppression Index (SSI) in detail

Component weights and graded severity

SSI is a 0-10 weighted sum of graded suppression severities: SSIt = Σ(weighti × severityit), severity ∈ [0,1] (1.0 = full suppression / discontinuation, ~0.7 = methodology dispute, ~0.3 = delay or post-resolution scar). There are six documented streams, and the weights sum to 10. The UPA years (2004-2013, shaded) record nothing, so SSI = 0. Both eras are computed identically in data/compute_indices.py. Cell values are the annual severity. No published third-party data is involved. These are severities coded by the author and anchored to datable events (see notes).

A4. Democratic Quality Index (DQI) calculations

Four-component geometric mean

DQI_t = (V_t × F_t × R_t × C_t)^(1/4) Where: V_t = V-Dem Liberal Democracy Index (0-1) F_t = Freedom House aggregate score / 100 R_t = (180 - RSF_rank_t) / 180 C_t = V-Dem Core Civil Society Index (0-1)

All four are published third-party measures. The Civil Society Index is added because press-freedom rank barely separates the eras (India was mediocre throughout), while civil-society space collapsed from 0.669 to 0.302 under the NDA. A headline democracy score flattens that dimension. V-Dem first classified India as an electoral autocracy in its Democracy Report of March 2021. The values shown for 2026 are V-Dem's 2025 observations carried forward, because V-Dem has published no 2026 figure.

A7. Robustness tests

Sensitivity analysis results

Test Base Result Alternative Specification Difference
SSI with ±20% weights 7.05 (2024) 5.6-8.5 ±20%
FCI excluding borrowing 0.44 (2024) 0.53 +20%
DQI arithmetic mean 0.28 (2024) 0.33 +18%
Note: No significance test lies behind these rows; each reports how much the 2024 value moves under the alternative. The SSI is a weighted sum, so scaling every weight by 20% scales the index by the same proportion and leaves the cross-era comparison unchanged. Excluding the borrowing component raises the 2024 FCI because that component is zero in every year except 2020 and 2021. Dropping both coded components (borrowing and GST) reverses the era ranking. On the four measured fiscal ratios alone the UPA years average 0.49 and the NDA years 0.45. A geometric mean penalizes weak dimensions more heavily than an arithmetic mean, which is why the arithmetic DQI is higher.

Summary statistics

  • GDP growth (2014-15 to 2025-26, 12 years): mean = 6.23%, SD = 4.02%; 7.32% excluding 2020-21. UPA decade (2004-05 to 2013-14): mean = 6.82%; 7.23% excluding 2008-09. Including the pandemic year the NDA average is lower, and excluding each era's worst year it is higher. 2023-24 onward is on the 2022-23 base released on 27 February 2026, and the earlier years on the 2011-12 base
  • CPI inflation (calendar-year averages): UPA 7.98%, NDA 4.80% (2014-2026; 2026 is January to August on the new 2024 base). Inflation was in double digits in 2009, 2010 and 2013 only
  • Unemployment: 2014 and 2016 have no published figure. 2015 (5.0%) is the Labour Bureau's 2015-16 survey, a different survey from PLFS. On PLFS usual status the rate fell from 6.0% (2017-18) to 3.1% (2025). The 2026 value (5.2%) is the average of the monthly bulletins on current weekly status, a different measure, so no mean is taken across the series
  • Top 1% Share: 21.3% (2014) to 22.6% (2022-23), +1.3pp, and 18.4% in 2004; the 2022-23 level is the highest recorded since 1922. Bottom 50% Share: 18.8% (2004) to 14.7% (2014), a low of 13.9% (2017), then 15.0% (2022-23). Era averages over published years: top 1% 20.39% (2004-2013) against 21.92% (2014-2022); bottom 50% 16.75% against 14.69%. No income share is published for 2023-2026
  • SSI: 0 across UPA; peaked 9.0 (2021-22, COVID undercount); 4.65 in 2026, with the census coded as a scheduled delay; NDA mean 4.79
  • FCI: UPA avg 0.30, NDA avg 0.43; 0.42 in 2014 (an accounting break in grants), peak 0.87 (2020), 0.35 (2026); on the four measured fiscal ratios alone, UPA 0.49 and NDA 0.45
  • DQI: fell from 0.49 (2014) to 0.28 (2026), a decline of 42.9%; UPA avg 0.57, NDA avg 0.37 (incl. civil-society index)
  • Press freedom: fell 17 places, from 140 (2014) to 157 (2026)
  • Devolution: rose from 27.1% of gross tax revenue (2014-15) to 33.9% (2024-25) (16th Finance Commission Annexure 7.1; Budget at a Glance 2026-27)
