India ranks 157th of 180 on press freedom

The RSF 2026 World Press Freedom Index, released 30 April, dropped India six places from 151 in 2025. India now sits below Pakistan (153), Bangladesh (152), and Nepal (87). RSF cites concentrated ownership, violence against journalists, and overt political alignment of outlets.

India's press environment is now judged worse than that of every immediate neighbour except Afghanistan.
