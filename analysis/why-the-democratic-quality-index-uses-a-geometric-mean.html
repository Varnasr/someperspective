Methodology

Why the Democratic Quality Index uses a geometric mean

DQI = (V-Dem Liberal Democracy Index × Freedom House score/100 × (180 - RSF rank)/180 × V-Dem Core Civil Society Index)^(1/4). A geometric mean is used so that a collapse in any single dimension drags the whole index down, and a country with strong elections but no press freedom is correctly penalised. An arithmetic mean would hide this.

Democratic quality is multiplicative. One weak pillar undermines the rest.
