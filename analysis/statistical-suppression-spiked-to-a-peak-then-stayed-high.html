Statistics

Statistical suppression spiked to a peak, then stayed high

SSI is a 0-10 weighted sum of graded suppression severities across six documented streams — suppressed surveys, delayed releases, methodology disputes, and statistical-body resignations. It was 0 across the UPA years, then peaked at 9.0 in 2021-22 as the COVID-mortality undercount (official ~0.5M deaths vs 3-5M excess) stacked on the census delay, the withheld consumption survey, the shelved GDP back-series and the permanent merger of the NSSO into the NSO, and remains at 6.4 in 2026 — even as the 2021 Census stayed incomplete.

A democratic state is being audited with less independent data than before — the acute events eased, but the institutional damage is permanent.
