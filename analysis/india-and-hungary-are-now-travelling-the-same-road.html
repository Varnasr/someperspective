India and Hungary are now travelling the same road

On the V-Dem Liberal Democracy Index: Hungary 0.521 (2014) → 0.315 (2025). India 0.488 → 0.260. The two began the period within 0.04 of each other and both ended near 0.3. India's fall of 0.228 points is slightly larger than Hungary's 0.206. V-Dem's latest observation is for 2025.

What follows. Hungary's score fell by a similar amount over the same years, and both countries continued to hold competitive elections.
