India and Hungary are now travelling the same road

On the V-Dem Liberal Democracy Index: Hungary 0.521 (2014) → 0.315 (2026). India 0.488 → 0.260. The two began the period within 0.04 of each other and both ended near 0.3; India's slide started later and travelled slightly further. The Hungary 2010-2024 trajectory has become the most-cited template for understanding India's path.

A democracy can fall this far while remaining electorally competitive — Hungary showed it first.
