Democracy

The Democratic Quality Index is the lowest it has been

DQI combines the V-Dem Liberal Democracy Index, Freedom House, the RSF rank and the V-Dem Core Civil Society Index via a geometric mean — so weakness in any single dimension pulls the composite down. It has fallen from 0.49 (2014) to 0.29 (2026), against a UPA-decade average of 0.57. The steepest single-year falls come after 2019.

No single index tells the whole story, but every credible index points the same direction.
