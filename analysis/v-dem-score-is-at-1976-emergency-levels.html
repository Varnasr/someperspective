Democracy

V-Dem score is at 1976-emergency levels

India's V-Dem Liberal Democracy score in 2026 (~0.26) is comparable to the score recorded during the 1975-1977 Emergency — when civil liberties were formally suspended. The current score has been reached without a formal Emergency declaration.

A democracy can be functionally undone without ever being formally suspended.
