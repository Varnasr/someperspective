Federalism

Cesses and surcharges reached a quarter of Union tax revenue in 2020-21

Cesses and surcharges are not shared with states under the Finance Commission formula. Their share of the Union's gross tax revenue was 10.4% in 2011-12 and 13.5% in 2014-15 (15th Finance Commission, Table 3.8). It rose to 19.9% in 2018-19, with the GST compensation cess included from 2017-18, and peaked at 24.4% in 2020-21 (20.2% without the compensation cess). It has fallen since, to 18.0% in 2024-25 and 14.3% in the 2026-27 budget estimate (Union Receipt Budgets).

Revenue raised as a cess stays with the Union, so the cess share measures how much of the tax take sits outside the pool that states share in.
