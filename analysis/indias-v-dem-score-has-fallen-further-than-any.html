Democracy

India's V-Dem score has fallen furthest in absolute terms

V-Dem Liberal Democracy Index: India 0.488 (2014) → 0.260 (2026), a 47% decline and a fall of 0.228 points. Brazil 0.783 → 0.704 (-10%). Turkey 0.252 → 0.110 (-56%). Hungary 0.521 → 0.315 (-40%). South Africa 0.641 → 0.633 (-1%). Turkey falls further in percentage terms, but from a base already near the floor; India's is the largest absolute fall in the cohort.

On the most-cited cross-country democracy index, India has erased four decades of liberalisation in one.
